# Game elements: the compendium, adapted for Knowledge Guardians

Mika shared a compendium of about 290 game elements, systems, minigame formats and side-quest kinds (2026-11-26). She wants all of them, together with the story elements ([STORY_ELEMENTS.md](STORY_ELEMENTS.md)) and the course content, woven through the game many times, in many worlds and chapters. Students learn through people, play and exploration. Nothing may feel like flashcards, a quiz, an essay, homework or an exam.

This file is the coverage table. Numbers are the compendium's item numbers. When you build or plan an element, move its number and name the file.

**Adaptation rules (apply to every element):**

- **Combat** becomes argument, investigation and checking: Showdowns, Fray creatures named as errors of reasoning, seals, crossings. Nobody is hurt.
- **No exam style.** Practice papers, readiness gauges, quiz shows and trivia duels (85, 161, 258) become play: calibration games, Tall Tales, Showdowns. Developer tests are fine; students never sit an exam.
- **No betting, gambling or theft.** Dice and cards are evidence and probability games. Stealth means watching quietly. A heist means an audit with permits.
- **No student-made content** (Mika, 2026-11-25). Level editors, student guidebooks and player hint messages (140, 159, 240) are not used.
- **Classroom only, private data.** The game has no server. Class-wide and multiplayer features (154 to 168) are not built; a teacher can run them offline with the in-game tools. Telemetry stays on the device.
- **Cultures.** Each people is its own; never group by region. Myths and patrons in play are invented and in-world.
- **Every element carries a TOK idea**, and its thinking level varies (DOK, Bloom, Fink, SOLO). See [LEARNING_DESIGN.md](LEARNING_DESIGN.md).

**Marks:** ✓ in the game · ◐ partly or in one place only · ○ planned · ✗ not used (reason given).
