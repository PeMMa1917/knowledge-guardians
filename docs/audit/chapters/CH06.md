# Chapter 6: The Metalworks

Chapter 6 is WORLD-07, the Metalworks Sample Yard and Trade Hall. Two blue panels are sold as a "matched" pair, but a balance gives two and four units. The keepers are Inspector Corra (tests) and Steward Bea (records and buyers). Real records include a Vindolanda tablet address and many real tools, from Henry Maudslay's micrometer to a Japanese saw set. The chapter has 15 missions, a standard-loop share of 0.27, and no field game of its own ([EVIDENCE.md](../EVIDENCE.md)).

Persona codes are in [PERSONAS.md](../PERSONAS.md). Fix numbers are in [CONSENSUS.md](../CONSENSUS.md). A tick (✅) means the fix is in the game.

## Whole chapter

### What most personas like
- **One word, two meanings.** "Matched" means a finish to painters and a workshop to sellers. C1, B1 and the language teachers (B3) like how a word carries a claim it has not earned.
- **A measurement that overturns a label.** Two and four units against "matched weight" is a clear, physical test (D3, B3 physics and chemistry).
- **The Second Turn (R10).** The player argues each side, then a listener says back what the notice would mean. Debate coaches (C3) and D1 call it the best argument mission so far.
- **Real tools from many workshops** (G2, G6, B3 design technology).

### What most personas are concerned about
- **Corra and Bea agree too quickly** (E2, G5). ✅ Fixed: four two-reply moments, after the choice of how to clip the readings and after the choice of where buyers see the evidence.
- **The Parts You Can Replace has 40 steps of "Verify a support for …"** (E1, A3, A5). ✅ Titles now say "Check that a holder fits …". The drawer grouping (C-010) is still planned.
- **One catalogue number left in a name** ("Ostracode image from Chimbote, USNM PAL 247790"). ✅ Fixed.
- **No field game.** The chapter is all records and benches (G8, H4). ✅ Fixed: The Assay Trial.
