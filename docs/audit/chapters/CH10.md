# Chapter 10: The Market of Measures

Chapter 10 is WORLD-11, a market square with a scale, a sample shed and a packing loft. A seller's tag says lot A gives more fibre for each credit, but the basket was weighed with the fibre. The keepers are Assayer Tellen and Clerk Dagny. The chapter has 14 missions, a standard-loop share of 0.14, and no field game of its own ([EVIDENCE.md](../EVIDENCE.md)).

Persona codes are in [PERSONAS.md](../PERSONAS.md). Fix numbers are in [CONSENSUS.md](../CONSENSUS.md). A tick (✅) means the fix is in the game.

## Whole chapter

### What most personas like

- **Only 14 missions and no game.** A market is the natural place for the planned **Value Fair** (G8, H4).
- **Religious welcome records need sources from the community** (J1, B8). The coordinator's note on Mika's rule: real faiths are shown with respect and sources; only a god as an opponent is invented.
