# Chapter 9: The House of Performance

Chapter 9 is WORLD-10, a theatre with a foyer, a rehearsal room, an audience gallery and a story archive. A worker pauses at a doorway before moving a crate, and the order of the pause and the move changes the reason the audience sees. The keepers are Director Edda and Host Hari. Real records include early photographic motion sequences, theatre images and many editions of stories. The chapter has 22 missions, a standard-loop share of 0.32, and no field game of its own ([EVIDENCE.md](../EVIDENCE.md)).

Persona codes are in [PERSONAS.md](../PERSONAS.md). Fix numbers are in [CONSENSUS.md](../CONSENSUS.md). A tick (✅) means the fix is in the game.

## Whole chapter

### What most personas like
- **Timing makes meaning.** "How does the timing of an action shape the reason an audience sees in it?" is a strong arts question, and the stake (the audience leaves sure of something it never saw) is clear (B3 theatre, C1, E1).
- **Seats as perspectives.** The front seats see the whole move; the side seats hear its rhythm. Perspective is a physical place (A7, H3, I1).
- **Editions and voices.** The story archive rooms separate a story, its edition and this particular copy (D7, B3 literature).

### What most personas are concerned about
- **Edda and Hari agree too much** (E2, G5). ✅ Fixed: four two-reply moments, after the cue order and after the choice of which seat view goes with the cast plan.
- **Four archive rooms in a row (R90 to R93) use the standard loop** and abstract titles (0.14 and 0.15) (E1, A4).
- **No field game.** A theatre is the natural place for play (G8, H4, E1).
- **A thinker would fit.** Verstehen (understanding a person from inside their situation) is planned for this chapter (DRIVE_WEAVE.md).
