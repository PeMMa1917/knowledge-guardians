# Chapter 7: The City of Copies

Chapter 7 is WORLD-08, a press yard, a lending hall and a market landing. A notice loses "Tuesday" at the press and the clerk's name at the landing, and readers walk to a closed hall. The keepers are Printer Lark and Lending Keeper Mina. Real records include an inscribed Shang oracle bone, the surviving second volume of Jikji (printed with metal type in 1377) and a red-headed vellum printing. The chapter has 25 missions, a standard-loop share of 0.32, and no field game of its own ([EVIDENCE.md](../EVIDENCE.md)).

Persona codes are in [PERSONAS.md](../PERSONAS.md). Fix numbers are in [CONSENSUS.md](../CONSENSUS.md). A tick (✅) means the fix is in the game.

## Whole chapter

### What most personas like
- **A claim that travels and loses its limits.** "What does a claim become when it travels without its limits or its source?" is the main TOK question of the whole game in one mission (B1, C1, D2).
- **Real printing history from Korea, China and Europe.** Jikji and the oracle bone stop the story of printing from starting with Gutenberg (D7, I4, J2).
- **Credit for the people who made a book** (D8, A7).
