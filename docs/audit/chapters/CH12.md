Chapter 12 is WORLD-13, a library dispatch with a callboard and a canal walk. A mobile library has four windows to share, and the dispatch rule decides which street gets a visit. The keepers are Keeper Ade and Route Clerk Lin. The chapter has 15 missions, a standard-loop share of 0.27, and no field game of its own ([EVIDENCE.md](../EVIDENCE.md)).
