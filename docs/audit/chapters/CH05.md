# Chapter 5: The Living Forest

Chapter 5 is WORLD-06. Birds cross a forest bridge, and two lookouts count them. Naturalist Elian keeps the lookouts and Steward Ayla keeps the bridge. Jane Goodall's early Gombe notes and the Gombe research team appear as real records about who gets credit for field work. The chapter has 20 missions, a standard-loop share of 0.15 (the lowest so far), and one field game, The Careful Dig ([EVIDENCE.md](../EVIDENCE.md)).

Persona codes are in [PERSONAS.md](../PERSONAS.md). Fix numbers are in [CONSENSUS.md](../CONSENSUS.md). A tick (✅) means the fix is in the game.

## Whole chapter

### What most personas like
- **Observer position as a real science problem.** Two lookouts at different heights give different counts, and the player has to decide what the count can show (D3, B3 biology, H3).
- **Credit for field work.** "Names in the Notebook" and "Two Hands on the Sheet" ask whose work disappears when a record keeps only the best-known name. D8 and I4 like that the Gombe research team, not only Jane Goodall, is credited.
- **Little standard loop.** The forest has the most hands-on missions so far: quiet watches, carrying a frame with a partner, a storm path (E1, G11).
- **The Careful Dig.** A site can be dug once, so digging is a choice about what later people can know (D7, B3).

### What most personas are concerned about
- **Elian and Ayla rarely disagree** about whose knowledge counts (E2, G5). ✅ Fixed: four two-reply moments, after the layout choice for the crews' notes and after the choice of how to send visitors.
- **Accession numbers in object names.** The fern record room showed "Pair of Stirrups, 42.50.466–467" and "Belt, 1939.25.a", and other rooms had nine more names like "Turtle Bell, Met 1977.187.26" (C-003). ✅ Fixed in all 12 names, and the audit test now catches a number inside a quoted name.
- **The making rooms (R86 to R89) have long steps** (long share 0.18 to 0.21), each about a schedule for one object (A3, A5).
- **The crews' local knowledge is a side note.** J1 and J2 ask that the lamp crew's knowledge of the reeds count as knowledge with its own method, not only as colour.
