# Chapter 13: The Archive of Voices

Chapter 13 is WORLD-14, an archive of spoken accounts with a glass walk and a listening room. A remembered walk must not be filed as a dated record, or the lantern opening is dated wrong for everyone after. The keepers are Listener Wren and Walk Clerk Eren. Real records include the Fort Wayne treaty (1809) and records about Tecumseh. Vesper's Seal works nearby. The chapter has 16 missions, a standard-loop share of 0.19, and no field game of its own ([EVIDENCE.md](../EVIDENCE.md)).

Persona codes are in [PERSONAS.md](../PERSONAS.md). Fix numbers are in [CONSENSUS.md](../CONSENSUS.md). A tick (✅) means the fix is in the game.

## Whole chapter

### What most personas like
- **Oral accounts as knowledge.** A local account and a written record do different jobs, and the archive keeps both (D7, J1, J2).
- **Trust after harm.** "How can a publisher earn back trust after a public claim has done harm?" (D1, C3).
- **A treaty and the people it affected.** The Fort Wayne treaty and Tecumseh's opposition to it give two sides of one record (D7, B3 history).

### What most personas are concerned about
- **Wren and Eren agree too much** (E2, G5). ✅ Fixed: four two-reply moments, after the sign wording and after where the approved excerpt goes.
- **Tecumseh's people need their own voice.** J1 and J2 ask that the Shawnee account comes from Shawnee sources (the Shawnee Tribe, the Absentee Shawnee Tribe and the Eastern Shawnee Tribe), not only from treaty papers (X-006, C-023).
- **The written record checks the oral one, but not the other way round** (C-024).
- **Three "Receiving Copy for …" missions (R49 to R51) have catalogue-like titles** (A4).
