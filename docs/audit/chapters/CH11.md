# Chapter 11: The Water Commons

Chapter 11 is WORLD-12, a shared weir with a council yard and seed rows. A drought plan must share ten buckets between the named rows and the lower channel. The keepers are Weirkeeper Omi and Clerk Sena. The chapter has 18 missions, a standard-loop share of 0.33, and no field game of its own ([EVIDENCE.md](../EVIDENCE.md)).

Persona codes are in [PERSONAS.md](../PERSONAS.md). Fix numbers are in [CONSENSUS.md](../CONSENSUS.md). A tick (✅) means the fix is in the game.
