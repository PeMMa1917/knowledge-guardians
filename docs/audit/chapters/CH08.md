# Chapter 8: The Cartographers’ Sea

Chapter 8 is WORLD-09, a harbour with a chartroom (Lantern House), a market rail and a shoal. A chart says the water was checked "to the page edge", but walkers stopped at Market Rail and the boat crew checked one strip. The keepers are Cartographer Renna and Harbourkeeper Sora. Real records include a Marshallese navigation object and cloth from Hawaiʻi. The chapter has 16 missions, a standard-loop share of 0.19, and no field game of its own ([EVIDENCE.md](../EVIDENCE.md)).

Persona codes are in [PERSONAS.md](../PERSONAS.md). Fix numbers are in [CONSENSUS.md](../CONSENSUS.md). A tick (✅) means the fix is in the game.

## Whole chapter

### What most personas like
- **What a map's blank spaces make people assume.** "If the chart hides its blank, a skiff runs onto the shoal" is one of the strongest stakes in the game (E1, D3, B3 geography).
- **Names carry work.** Carriers say "Seagrass Steps"; the boat crew starts from Lantern House. Language teachers (B3) and J2 like that a place name shows how its speakers move through the place.
- **Dates on one object's history** ("The Beside Date", "Two Dates at a Peg") (D7, B3 history).

### What most personas are concerned about
- **Renna and Sora agree too much** (E2, G5). ✅ Fixed: four two-reply moments, after the place-names choice and after the front-of-display choice.
- **The Marshallese object is only a learning aid.** J1 and J2 ask that the Marshallese navigation tradition is shown as knowledge with its own method, with sources from the Marshallese people (X-006).
- **"The Address for the Question" (R24) is long and abstract** (0.2 and 0.1), and it asks about reuse authority in one step (A3, A4).
- **No field game** (G8, H4).
