## How much is left?

The first milestone is one source-backed gameplay example for each micro-skill. **1,177 of 1,200** still need one. Of these, 596 have an objective to inspect. Another 581 need a gameplay link established and tested. A suitable existing encounter can supply that link.

Keeping chapters separate, there are 432,000 micro-skill × chapter × Bloom × DOK positions. **26 have a documented action and 431,974 remain open.** One action per position would clear this particular count. The requested ten distinct actions per exact combination is a further requirement.

Including one of the 150 story elements and one of the 288 game elements yields 18,662,400,000 positions with a fixed TOK context. Varying one key concept, one framework part and a single area or theme raises this subtotal to 9,853,747,200,000. Larger sets, knowledge questions and ordered routes expand it further. One encounter can support multiple overlapping selections. These counts describe review positions, rather than a count of scenes to write. Orders with unlimited repetition have no finite total.

## Time and credit estimate for the first milestone

Together these assumptions give roughly **730–2,041 person-hours**, or about 18–51 forty-hour weeks. The estimate covers one documented opportunity per micro-skill. It assumes completed routes can supply some evidence immediately. New mechanics, historical source work, classroom trials and native-device validation can increase the effort. Parallel production and shared encounters can change elapsed time.

For credits, use two representative batches on the intended model and account. Let R be the billed credits for reviewing ten already-linked skills and N the billed credits for establishing and testing ten new links. The first-pass budget is approximately **59.6 × R + 58.1 × N credits**. Account usage supplies R and N. Test counts and checked cells do not determine a billing rate. The exhaustive specification has no credible fixed time or credit estimate.
