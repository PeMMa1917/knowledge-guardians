# Two coats for one fare

Start at any of the four rails. Each offers two physical checks. Carry the results between rooms or try both at one place. Together they open a hearing about how the next borrower gets a coat. Every settlement has a later shift in each room. The quiet hook opens a new room behind the Nexus rail. A reach or lining observation can also reveal its door. The return to the archive is always available from inside.

## Four beats of indoor rain

Find the bulb and folded sleeve on the quiet landing or in the theatre foyer. There are two drops and four beats. Catch a drop in the cuff, drip directly into the cup, tip the cuff or leave a rest. Keep one sound from the stalls. Try to produce it another way. The same third-beat tap can come from a stored drop or a direct squeeze. A listener can hear the tap. A worker in the wings can see which hand released it.

Send a programme with a named bow or an open demonstration. The other room receives the last issued score, even while someone tries a new one on the first rig. The receiving player can repeat it exactly or choose a different billing. The diary records the chosen gestures, vantage point, decision and transfer.
