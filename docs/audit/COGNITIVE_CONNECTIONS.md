# What a connection looks like in play

The combination atlas extends this audit to multiple members of the same family and ordered routes. [Combination atlas](COMBINATION_ATLAS.md)

At the well, a porter needs a pot he can lift one-handed. At the council, a listener needs three knocks entered in a record. The player can send a speech, token or messenger rule at either of two bells. The council tries the actual rule that arrived. Its successor changes the porter's next turn back in Emberford.

The audit records the act, the demand it places on the player and a consequence that can be observed. The historical meaning of *evidence* earns a link when the player considers a rubbing alongside a sound and decides what the hatch will receive. Reading the word alone earns no credit for that decision.

## Reproduce a check

```sh
node tools/audit-cognitive-weave.mjs --unit chapter --evidence --json
node tools/audit-cognitive-weave.mjs --world WORLD-02 --dimension etymology:claim --story 11 --game 114 --bloom create --dok 3 --evidence
node tools/audit-cognitive-weave.mjs --scope all --unit campaign --dimension subject:geography --evidence
```

Each result includes the number of possible connections, the number with a reviewed action, and the number with ten distinct actions. Repeating a route leaves the count alone. The same action must demonstrate the particular curriculum element, narrative element, game element, Bloom level and depth being claimed. A separate view checks a narrative or game element by itself. World, chapter and campaign groupings answer different questions. The fifteen chapters contain sixteen worlds.

The repertoire includes the fallacies and biases in the supplied posters and ten forms of deliberate deception used as review categories. Terms with multiple names share an entry. The catalogue is bounded. A lost line, an empty record and an inaccurate recollection each permit several explanations for intent. The audit requires separate support for crediting deliberate deception. The full population includes planned and excluded game elements. Counts measure traceable opportunities in the current game. Player enjoyment and transfer to a new real situation require observations of players.
