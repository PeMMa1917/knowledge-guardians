## Decisions that remain in tension

- A collector can want a complete checklist while a reluctant reader wants one satisfying evening. The first delivery works with one companion and one object. Finishing the whole gallery is optional.
- Some examiners want explicit analysis. Some players prefer to stay inside the fiction. The witnesses tell their stories in the room. Optional diary prompts invite a more explicit account.
- A curator values a fixed accession and title. A cultural reviewer may question how the collection assigned them. Keep the record available and identify whose attribution it is. Real community reviewers can propose more precise, sourced readings.
- Authors may want a definitive ending. Other reviewers value continuing disagreement. Each signed ending keeps the names and editions that led to it, even if the player later hears the other one.

The review does not establish presentation on physical iOS, Android or managed Chromebooks, student enjoyment or transfer, acceptance by any named community, or ten demonstrated uses of every skill–element pairing in each world.
