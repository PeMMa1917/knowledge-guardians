# Work orders in the fifteen halls

Activate the travelling case shelf in a main hall. Open “Browse field games, requests and missions” and choose a shelf. “Try a different kind of work” favours an unissued order with a different action and keeper. The directory, search and manual choice remain available. An order keeps its progress if the Guardian leaves before finishing it.

The five work shelves use 40 field-game forms, 30 side-quest forms, 12 main approaches, 36 mission forms and 42 pattern forms. Each of the forty field forms has its own playable instrument. A circuit changes the lamps when a wired pair is switched. A grower can shade one seed row while holding its partner steady. The remaining shelves use eight general rehearsal shapes and twelve evidence instruments. These encounters share controls, openings and character lines. The counts above describe different orders and stops. They cannot establish how often a class will experience repetition. The older main missions retain their own play systems.

## Following an order

In the first hall, choose the Tompion repeater case. Load the fictional carrier and watch which way it pulls. Matching the pull opens two dispatch choices with different costs for the carrier and clerk. A witness hearing plays differently. Decide where the visitor speaks and what can leave the room. The two decisions have different consequences for the witness. In a ferry order, move around the obstruction and reach the far landing. A failed move leaves the boat in place.

Some comparative orders require a forecast and two readings. An inaccurate forecast can go in the diary and the keeper can still issue a decision. Some rivals will have their objection travel with the finding if heard after a rehearsal. The guardian’s gift can reveal the otherwise untested third setting. Twelve Civic Machine source rows had empty object labels. Their orders use named fictional slips, markers and fittings from the local workplace.

An issued order with a destination enters the receiving world's “Incoming work” shelf. Opening the crossing uses that world's normal arrival state. All destinations respect the act and Council gates. Revising the disposition makes a previous receipt visibly out of date. Each shelf loads separately.

## Evidence

The automated walk applies the public game controls to all 30,240 local orders, finds a working path through each instrument and delivers every addressed order. It checks every local world for eight rehearsal shapes and a maximum share below 40% for any one shape in this collection. Separate checks cover forecast order, missing gifts, rival access, revisions, saved state, journal topics and repeat avoidance. These checks cover feasibility and measured variety. Student playtesting is still needed to assess pacing and emotional engagement.
