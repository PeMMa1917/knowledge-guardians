# Voice sheets

This file gives each named character a voice (audit fix C-005). Use it when you write a new line for a character. Each sheet has four parts:

- **Believes:** what the character trusts or wants.
- **Worries:** the mistake the character fears most.
- **Speaks:** how the character talks.
- **Why:** where the belief comes from, in the story.

Two keepers in one chapter must disagree for good reasons (decision X-008). Their beliefs are built to pull against each other. Neither keeper is right (OPEN-001).

A test (`tests/voices.test.ts`) checks that every character with more than 20 lines of dialogue has a sheet here. Put the character id in backticks in the heading, as below.

## The Nexus

### Warden Ise Marrow (`warden-ise`)
- **Believes:** a label must not say more than its evidence supports.
- **Worries:** a reader who repeats a label as if it were a finding.
- **Speaks:** short, calm sentences. Gives one task at a time. Rarely jokes.
- **Why:** she trains new Guardians, and she has seen the Gilded Seal turn a tidy label into a public mistake.

### Curator Mireille Dace (`curator-dace`)
- **Believes:** a display is for the visitor who has five seconds.
- **Worries:** a kind, short label that leaves out who did the work.
- **Speaks:** tells small stories about visitors. Admits her own old mistakes.
- **Why:** she runs the display hall and watches people read it every day.

### Clerk Tobiah Venn (`clerk-venn`)
- **Believes:** a date with its source is something anyone can check later.
- **Worries:** a record that only the person on duty can find.
- **Speaks:** exact. Reads dates and numbers aloud. Points with his thumb.
- **Why:** he keeps the Nexus records and works shifts, so others must use his records without him.

### Archivist Noor Tull (`archivist-tull`)
- **Believes:** every claim should answer the question "says who?".
- **Worries:** "everyone knows", the start of every bad label.
- **Speaks:** dry and precise. Names the keywords that find a record.
- **Why:** the archive holds the documents that the labels quote.

### Kip Arden (`kip-arden`)
- **Believes:** questions come first, and one question can lead to three more.
- **Worries:** being left out, and finishing nothing.
- **Speaks:** fast, cheerful, interrupts himself. Shares what he finds.
- **Why:** a new Guardian like the player, who learns by asking.

### Vesper Quill (`vesper-quill`)
- **Believes:** one large, clear word can do a lot of work fast.
- **Worries:** a page too long for anyone to read before the bell.
- **Speaks:** smooth and charming. Speaks in neat phrases. Sometimes regrets them.
- **Why:** a former Guardian who leads the Gilded Seal. The Seal's short labels did real harm.
